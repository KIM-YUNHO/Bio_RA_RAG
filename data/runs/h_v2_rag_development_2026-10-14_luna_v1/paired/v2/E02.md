## 쟁점 T1

EMA CT-P43 1.2 건강인 PK 시험의 대상군과 Day -1 층화 조건을 원문 기준으로 정리해줘. 일본인 여부, 성별, 체중 구간을 구분해줘.

### C1.1 · 원문 사실

CT-P43 1.2는 일본에서 실시된 건강인 시험으로, 등록된 모든 대상자는 일본인 남성이었다.

- 출처: Steqeyma : EPAR - Public assessment report · p.88 · 513959da3a6d724b63160792cbf18acb7f83d3396ab8147f491d543ce392bcce
- 원문: Study CT-P43 1.2: Healthy subjects

- 출처: Steqeyma : EPAR - Public assessment report · p.88 · 513959da3a6d724b63160792cbf18acb7f83d3396ab8147f491d543ce392bcce
- 원문: This study was conducted in Japan, with a total of 331 subjects randomly assigned to a treatment group. Of the 331 subjects, 14 (4.2%) subjects withdrew or discontinued from the study (5 [4.4%], 5 [4.5%] and 4 [3.7%] subjects in the CT-P43, EU-Stelara and US-Stelara group, respectively). Withdrawal by subject was the most frequently reported primary reason for study discontinuation (4 [3.5%], 4 [3.6%] and 3 [2.8%] subjects, respectively). Thus, 325 subjects (111, 108 and 106 subjects in the CT-P43, EU-Stelara and US-Stelara group, respectively) received a single dose (45 mg) of study drug. All subjects enrolled were Japanese males with mean age of 34.8 years and generally similar demographic and baseline characteristics across treatment groups.

### C2.1 · 원문 사실

대상자는 시험기관과 Day -1 체중에 따라 층화되었으며, 체중 허용 범위는 저체중 50~&lt;60 kg, 중간 60~&lt;70 kg, 고체중 70~&lt;80 kg, 최고체중 80~90 kg(90 kg 포함)이었다.

- 출처: Steqeyma : EPAR - Public assessment report · p.39 · 513959da3a6d724b63160792cbf18acb7f83d3396ab8147f491d543ce392bcce
- 원문: Study CT-P43 1.2 was a Phase I, Randomised, Double-Blind, Parallel Group, Single-Dose Study to Compare the Pharmacokinetics, Safety, and Immunogenicity of Three Subcutaneous Injection Formulations of Ustekinumab (CT-P43, EU-approved Stelara, and US-licensed Stelara) in Healthy Japanese Male Subjects.

- 출처: Steqeyma : EPAR - Public assessment report · p.39 · 513959da3a6d724b63160792cbf18acb7f83d3396ab8147f491d543ce392bcce
- 원문: The study was planned to be conducted in 327 subjects. The 327 subjects were to be enrolled to demonstrate PK similarity and randomised in a 1:1:1 ratio to receive a single dose (45 mg/0.5 mL) of CT - P43, EU-approved Stelara or US-licensed Stelara. All subjects received a single dose of study drug subcutaneously via PFS on Day 1 and were followed up for 113 days for PK, safety and immunogenicity assessments. Subjects were stratified by study centre and body weight at Day -1 (low: 50 to &lt;60 kg; medium: 60 to &lt;70 kg; high: 70 to &lt;80 kg; and very high: 80 to 90 kg, 90 inclusive) to allow for balanced weight distribution.
