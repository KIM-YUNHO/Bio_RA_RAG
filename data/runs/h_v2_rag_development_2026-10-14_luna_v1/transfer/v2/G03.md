- 원문: Yesintek has demonstrated a comparable safety profile to that of the reference biologic drug, Stelara. Therefore, the Adverse Reactions section of the Product Monograph for Yesintek is based on clinical experience with Stelara.

### C3.1 · 기관 판단

Yesintek의 허가된 적응증은 성인 및 6~17세 소아의 판상 건선, 성인 건선성 관절염, 성인 크론병 및 성인 궤양성 대장염이다.

- 출처: Summary Basis of Decision for Yesintek and Yesintek I.V. · p.8 · ca45114e8702a184915731e0915211f7922db53fc95060383636886d3ed3de4c
- 원문: Within this New Drug Submission, the sponsor requested authorization of Yesintek for the same indications and clinical uses currently authorized for Stelara. Based on the reviewed data, the benefit-risk profile of Yesintek is considered to be similar to the known benefit-risk profile of the reference biologic drug. Therefore, the benefit-risk profile of Yesintek is considered favourable for the treatment of plaque psoriasis in adults and in pediatric patients aged 6 to 17 years, as well as psoriatic arthritis in adults, Crohn’s disease in adults, and ulcerative colitis in adults. As with Stelara, appropriate warnings and precautions are in place in the Product Monograph for Yesintek to address the identified safety concerns.
